# Selya — reference-led sculpt v002

The unchanged `public/assets/companions/fairy.png` governs Selya's identity.
Hidden surfaces are inferred from that single illustration. All historical
figures and the delivered Caelo/Liora masters remain untouched.

## Modeled changes

- Rebuilt warm-toned face, cupped pointed ears, articulated hands and visible
  separate toes; recessed teal eyes with individually moving lid covers.
- Continuous scored coral hair foundation, overlapping crown waves, long side
  locks and lower curls, with separate gold botanical crown jewelry.
- Four closed pearl-mint wing membranes with modeled branched gold veins.
- Three tiers of folded coral, ivory and sage petal clothing, fine gold margins
  and vein geometry, fitted bodice, belt gem and leaf-shaped cuffs.
- Surface-fitted sandal straps, separate soles and faceted ankle fittings.

The local `model.py` builds actual volume, with no projected reference image.
The editable Blender master retains named separate parts; only weighted export
copies are merged into one self-contained Meshopt GLB. Density was reduced in
hidden anatomy and oversampled small details without removing the wing branches.

## Reproduction and evidence

Use the [shared v002 workflow](../../companions/sculpt-v002/README.md), selecting
`--kind fairy`, and a fresh absolute output directory. Blender 5.2.1 LTS and its
bundled tools suffice; no paid service or additional dependency is required.
The common nine clips remain idle, blink, happy, eat, play, pet_reaction, sleep,
wake and walk. They are gentle presentation gestures, not simulated flight,
cloth or biomechanical motion.

The manifest and [review](../../../reference/fairy/sculpt-v002/REVIEW.md) record
actual export, independent import and sampled-motion evidence. Source rendering,
technical validation, browser loading and human likeness approval are distinct.
Broad hair locks and the botanical dress remain stylized interpretations, not
an exact 1:1 reconstruction. Representative mobile performance is unmeasured.
Default 2D and reduced-motion/error fallbacks remain. No authentication change,
deployment or Git push is part of this local, project-controlled handoff.
