# Asterion likeness study v005

This editable revision uses the preserved v004 master and original four-view
turnaround. `likeness.py` changes crown/ear proportions, cheeks/nose, iris pigment,
upper-chest silhouette, scale relief, rear collar fit and golden claws. The
original rest skeleton and all nine actions remain unchanged; body and armor
retain their separate shared-skin components.

Head mane remains omitted under the user's earlier temporary instruction.
Native tail hair is retained. The chest silhouette field transforms strand
centers while preserving circular cross-sections; native curves and portable
tube rings are independently compared after reopening. Ocular vertices stay at
their original lid-bone pivots to avoid introducing a partial-blink offset.
The closed-cover surfaces are fitted to the actual curved eyes; catchlights
are seated on their corneal support so they do not protrude through the lids.
This changes geometry, not the original blink keys or action timing.

Use `--kind asterion` with the [shared likeness builder](../../companions/sculpt-v004/README.md).
`manifest.json` and the [validation report](../../../reference/asterion/sculpt-v005/validation.json)
bind the source, GLB, original reference and fresh source/import renders.

This is a further art study, not a 100-percent visual match, new animation pass,
mobile asset, inventory system, deployment or approved final likeness.
Every earlier revision is preserved.
